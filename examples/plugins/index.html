Effect plugins

Pick an effect: it is applied to the same live HTML form drawn inside the canvas below, and the form stays usable through it. The effects host, the plugins and the frame cap are extensions of this library, not part of the HTML-in-Canvas proposal; browsers' native implementations do not provide them (details). Link to one effect with ?effect=<name>.

    Real DOM, drawn by a polyfill

    Touch the interface.

    Every field you see is ordinary HTML inside this canvas. The selected effect is a WebGPU plugin applied to the drawn result; the form keeps its layout, focus, typing and scrolling, and clicks are mapped through the effect to the field you see.
