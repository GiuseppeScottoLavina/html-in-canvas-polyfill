Water form

A real HTML form drawn into a canvas by hic-live-polyfill, then presented through a WebGPU water-ripple plugin. Move the pointer over it to make ripples. The fields stay live: click one, type, press Submit. Drag the frame cap slider (15 to 120 fps) to limit how often the effect renders.
